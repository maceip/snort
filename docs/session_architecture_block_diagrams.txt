================================================================================
SNORT: TEMPORAL ARCHITECTURE & RESEARCH INTEGRATION BLOCK DIAGRAMS
================================================================================
These diagrams preserve a research design sketch for text-mode review.
Timing, memory, and scaling figures are illustrative targets, not measurements.
The mmap sidecar, Lance ANN index, loop coalescing, and automatic live attribution
and causal reconstruction are proposed. Current helpers use keyword tagging,
in-memory trigrams, exact cosine scans, and lookup-table edge scoring. The live
caller does not provide reciprocal degrees to the queue. Consult live-api.md and
research-and-temporal-architecture-review.md for the current source audit.
